>> 		// x[i] - x[i+1] = answer[i] - answer[i+1]

for (int i=0; i<n-1; i++)
   post(this, x[i] - x[i+1] == answer[i] - answer[i+1]);

should do the trick (you have to include gecode/minimodel.hh for this
to work).
